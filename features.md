1. Savings Circles (Ajo / Esusu / Adashe)
The cultural norm in Nigeria. A group of N people each contribute the same amount on a schedule, and one person receives the whole pot each round. MOVA automates collection, rotation order, and release timing.

Why it fits: Same core mechanic (money held → released on a rule), but the rule involves other people. It's the natural "group" version of Trustee Wallets.

Effort: High. Needs KYC per participant, dispute resolution, and a payout engine.

Differentiator: Almost nobody has done this well with the wallet-control mechanics you already have.



5. Cooling-off period for early breaks
If a user breaks a wallet early, don't release the money instantly. Queue it for 24–48 hours and let them cancel. This single feature is pure behavioral economics — many impulse breaks will be cancelled.

Why it fits: Your entire product is about slowing down money. This extends the same idea to the undo button.

Effort: Low.

Differentiator: Surprisingly rare. Most apps release instantly.
